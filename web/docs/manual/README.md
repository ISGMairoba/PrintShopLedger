First text draft · 15 October 2026

The examples use **Sample Print Shop** and **Training Customer A**. These are fictional training records, not live customers or price recommendations. Practise in a separate training workspace or the local trial; do not create training sales in your live business.

## Before following a chapter

- Your shop may rename pages and terms, so your labels may differ from the default labels used here.
- Your available pages and actions depend on the business's permissions. A missing page is not necessarily an error.
- A calculation, a quotation and a completed checkout are different actions. Only complete a checkout when you intend to record the sale or payment.
- Saving and printing are separate. A printer problem does not mean the sale failed.
