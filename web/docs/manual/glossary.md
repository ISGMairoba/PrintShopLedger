# Plain-language glossary

[Manual contents](README.md)

| Term | Meaning in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Business / workspace | The shop whose customers, stock and financial records you are working with. |
| Staff account | Your own username/password identity inside the business. It identifies who performed an action. |
| Service | A catalogue entry for work sold by the shop. It can be linked to material or be materialless. |
| Retail / shelf item | A catalogue product sold as an item rather than a production service. |
| Material | Physical stock tracked in a measurement unit, such as sheets or metres. |
| Quantity / size | The number of units and, where required, dimensions used to calculate the charge and material consumption. |
| Job | An individual order line with its own charge, outstanding payment balance and applicable production stage. |
| Checkout | A saved selling/payment operation. It can contain several new jobs, payments toward earlier jobs, or a payment without a new job. |
| Money received | New money actually collected in that operation. Existing wallet credit is shown separately. |
| Payment allocation | The part of a payment assigned to a particular job. Automatic allocation pays older unpaid jobs first. |
| Outstanding | The amount still unpaid. A job's outstanding amount and the customer's combined outstanding amount can differ. |
| Wallet / customer credit | Money held on the customer's account for later use or an authorised refund. It is not another sale. |
| Credit limit | The level of customer debt at which a warning appears. The warning itself does not stop the order being saved. |
| Selling price | What the shop charges the customer for the item or service. |
| Template cost | An internal expense rate used to calculate a template's cost. It is not necessarily the selling price. |
| Private cost | A template-only cost entry that does not appear as a sellable service at checkout. |
| Pricing template | Reusable inputs and calculations that work out a price and, where configured, stock consumption. |
| Markup | Profit expressed as a percentage of cost. Cost ₦1,000 with 20% markup gives a price of ₦1,200. |
| Margin | Profit expressed as a percentage of selling price. Cost ₦1,000 with 20% margin gives a price of ₦1,250, before any separate rounding rule. |
| Quotation | A proposed price for the customer. Saving it does not record payment or deduct stock. |
| Receipt | A document for a recorded transaction. Later authorised adjustments can be shown without rewriting the original transaction totals. |
| Completed / collected | Completed means production is finished. Collected means the work has been taken by the customer. These are separate stages. |
| Stock addition / count correction | An addition increases the current stock balance. A physical-count correction sets a checked balance. |
| Expense / expense payment | An expense records an incurred business cost; its payment records money paid toward it. They may happen on different dates. |
| Correction / cancellation / refund | A correction changes an authorised record; cancellation removes the job's charge/stops production as applicable; a refund records money returned. These are not interchangeable. |
| Drawer handover | A recorded transfer between staff, including expected cash, counted cash, any difference and confirmation. Switching user alone is not a handover. |
| Pending / synced | Pending work is saved on the current device and awaits cloud processing. Synced work has been confirmed by the server. |
| Conflict / review | Work that needs a decision because a related record changed elsewhere. It is not a request to collect the payment again. |
| Archive | Stop using a catalogue/customer/user record for new work while retaining its history, subject to permissions. |
| Local trial | A separate practice workspace on the current device. Its records do not become live business records. |

Your business may rename some terms. Ask a supervisor which local names correspond to these default labels.
